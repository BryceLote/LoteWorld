Identity & Character Sheet

Concept

An immovable frontline fighter that wins by not losing. It layers physical mitigation, magical resilience, and passive sustain so combat needs almost no reactive input: stand ground, absorb punishment, outlast.

Sword-and-board Heavy Armor with a blunt weapon (Skull Crusher), prioritizing consistency and durability, with god-tier burst available on demand through Alchemy. The win condition: enemy damage collapses against stacked mitigation while passive Restore Health refills the gap.

How It Plays

three independent defensive axes
Physical
Resist Normal Weapons 65 on top of Heavy Armor 100 (283 armor rating) and Block 100 with a shield guts melee damage.
Magic
Spell Absorption 25% rolls first (nullify + gain magicka), then Reflect 45% (bounce back at the caster) — roughly a 59% chance any hostile spell does zero and may punish the caster, before resistances touch the remainder. Fire is then soaked entirely.
Sustain
Constant Restore Health plus Eleidon's Ward (50–100 burst heal, cast when used) means you outlast rather than out-DPS.

Design Highlights

why it's smart
  • Layered anti-magic is the standout: absorption, then reflection, then resistance. Enemy mages effectively fight themselves.
  • Fire 125 is deliberate over-engineering: 25 pts of overflow past the practical 100% cap buffers against enemy fire-weakness debuffs. Pre-paid insurance.
  • Nord racial synergy covers the two elements the gear doesn't (Shock 70 total, Frost immune), freeing gear slots to over-invest in fire.
  • Feather 115 is the quiet MVP: it holds encumbrance near 0/600 despite a full Daedric loadout, keeping him mobile instead of turtled.
  • Artifact efficiency: Oreyn Bearclaw (Agi 40 + End 40) is near best-in-slot; Fists of Randagulf double-dip Str + Agi from the glove slot; the two rings cover Reflect / RNW / Acrobatics and Poison / Shock / Magicka.

Offense

defense is the baseline; offense scales on tap

Despite reading as a pure defensive shell on paper, Brutus hits hard, and can hit like a god on command.

Attributes maxed before gear, so gear Fortify stacks on capped stats: Strength 120, Agility 160. Since Strength is a linear multiplier on melee damage in Morrowind, high blunt damage is baked in, backed by Blunt Weapon 100 and Block 100.

Alchemy 71 + Fortify Intelligence looping = on-demand god mode. Drinking Fortify Intelligence potions balloons Magicka and potion strength, letting him brew massively fortified combat potions (Fortify Strength / Attack / Blunt). The stats screen captured this mid-loop: Intelligence and Magicka at ~10,923. When a fight needs to end now, a potion or two makes damage effectively unlimited.

Net: defense is the always-on baseline; offense scales from "solidly strong" to "god-tier" at will.

Honest Weaknesses

  • Spell Absorption is indiscriminate: it can eat your own self-cast buffs and heals. The build sidesteps this by being gear- and potion-driven (constant Restore Health + cast-when-used shield), so it's a non-issue as long as you don't self-cast defensive magic.
  • Resist Normal Weapons has gaps: it excludes enchanted/Daedric enemy weapons and magical creature attacks. Heavy Armor + shield + high health cover those, but it's the one crack in the physical wall.

Overall Assessment

A near-optimal constant-effect defensive tank, close to the set a theorycrafter would converge on, with a couple of genuinely sharp touches: the absorption→reflection→resist magic stack, the fire overflow buffer, the Nord racial covering the off-elements. With attributes maxed underneath (Str 120 / Agi 160 / End 140), four combat skills at 100, and Alchemy for on-demand burst, Brutus is not the slow-to-kill glass fortress he looks like on paper. He's a 283-armor wall that also swings hard and can spike to god-tier whenever he wants. Planned on paper, followed exactly, and it does what it set out to do: it makes endgame Morrowind an attrition problem you always win.
